Free Body Diagram Rules

  1. Draw a box, on a surface or in the air.
  2. Draw & label each force acting on the object. Arrows start at the box and point the way the force pushes or pulls.
  3. Arrow length shows the relative magnitude. Equal forces get equal arrows.
  4. Balanced: forces in either direction cancel. Fnet = 0 N; No Direction.
  5. Unbalanced: Fnet has a size and a direction.
  6. Forces are not required to maintain motion. Only draw forces from things touching the object (plus gravity).
